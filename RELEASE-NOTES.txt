NetRadiant Custom - Large Maps

Features
- Linked .map instances for mapper collaboration, expanded by the bundled q3map2.
- Dockable Map Instances panel with visibility, source status and dates,
  bulk add/remove, source opening, focus and refresh controls.
- See docs/map-instancing.md for usage and current translation-only limits.
- Simulated Map Lights preview with dynamic-light and sun shadows.
- Beer-Lambert water light absorption in q3map2.
- q3map2 riverbed precomputation and steady-state river water baking for sailing rivers.
- Large Maps performance options for loading, viewing, editing, and compiling.

Bug fixes
- Fixed 3D Ctrl+click face-selection highlighting with Batched static geometry enabled.
